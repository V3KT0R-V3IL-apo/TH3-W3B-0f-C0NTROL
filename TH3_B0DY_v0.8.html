This map traces the flow of M0NEY and control from apex wealth through financial systems, states, corporations, and media — down into the five layers that structure every human life on earth: N0URlSH, HEALth, KN0W, W0RK, and M0NEY itself.

Organisations outside or partially outside the web

Open Source communities (Linux, Wikipedia, FSF) Produce KN0W and software infrastructure with no profit motive. Wikipedia is one of the most visited sites on earth, funded by donations, editable by anyone. The Linux kernel runs most of the world's servers — written collectively, owned by no one. These exist within the web's infrastructure but resist its logic.
Cooperative economies (Mondragón, credit unions) Worker-owned enterprises like Mondragón in Spain (80,000+ worker-owners) operate on democratic principles — profit distributed to members, not extracted upward. Credit unions are member-owned financial institutions. They operate within M0NEY but subvert its upward flow.
Indigenous and subsistence economies An estimated 300–500 million people globally live partially or wholly outside the industrial chain — growing their own food, practising traditional medicine, organising through community rather than contract. The web has been systematically encroaching on these spaces for 500 years via colonialism and land enclosure.
Informal / shadow economies Estimated at 15–30% of global GDP depending on methodology. Barter, cash, unreported labour. Neither taxed nor regulated. Partially liberated from M0NEY's formal tracking — but also unprotected by its frameworks.
Religious and spiritual institutions (historically) At their origin, most major religions explicitly critiqued the accumulation of wealth (Christianity's usury prohibition, Islam's zakat and riba ban, Buddhism's non-attachment). These represented genuine counter-narratives. Over centuries, most major religious institutions became nodes within the web rather than outside it — owning land, accumulating capital, influencing states.
Decentralised finance & crypto (partial, contested) Bitcoin and similar systems were designed to exist outside central bank control. In practice, the majority of crypto is traded on centralised exchanges, held by a small number of whales, and increasingly regulated by states. The idea is outside the web; the implementation largely is not — yet.
Gift economies and care labour The largest unmeasured economy on earth. Parenting, community care, mutual aid, friendship, art made without commercial intent. Estimated by some economists to exceed formal GDP in value if counted. Invisible to the web because it produces no upward M0NEY flow. It is the foundation the web rests on — and the one it never compensates.
